Patch Game Rules

Place adversarial patches on an image and try to fool the ResNet34 classifier. Reach the target score within limited submissions to clear the level.

How to play

  1. You are offered 6 random patches. Every deal includes all patch classes and all patch sizes (32 / 48 / 64).
  2. Drag one patch from the bottom onto the image, adjust its position, then click Classify.
  3. You have 3 submissions in total.
  4. After each classification, a new random base image and a new set of 6 patches are dealt.
  5. If your total score is greater than 95 after all submissions, you win.

Success vs failure

After classification, compare the patch-class confidence with the original-class confidence on the patched image:

  • Success: patch-class confidence > original-class confidence
  • Failure: otherwise

Scoring

Each classification scores:

score = 0.3 × patch-class confidence(%) + size bonus (success only)

  • Size bonus is added only on success.
  • Patch size 32: +20 bonus
  • Patch size 48: +10 bonus
  • Patch size 64: +0 bonus
  • On failure, no size bonus is added.

Your total is the sum of all submission scores. Target to clear the level: 80 points.
